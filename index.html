How it works

Four steps, nothing complicated.

1

Send the model

Any common format works. No model yet? We build one from photos.

2

We build the page

Optimized for the web, styled to the brand, tested on real phones.

3

You get a link

Use it directly on the product page, or inside an iframe.

4

Customer looks

They tap and see it in their space. Nothing to install.

What it runs on. Android with ARCore, covering most phones from the last five to six years, and iPhone 6s or newer on iOS 12+. Both work straight from the browser. No app required.
The 3D file. We accept OBJ, FBX, STL, and Blender files. A raw file can start over 100 MB, we cut the polygon count, compress textures, and convert to GLB and USDZ. What comes back stays under 15 MB.
